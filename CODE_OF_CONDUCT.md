# Code of Conduct

This organization expects direct, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, specific, and useful.
- Assume technical disagreement is normal.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Repeated off-topic demands after maintainers have made a decision.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a block from the organization.
